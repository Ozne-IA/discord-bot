2. Message content

The Bot reads message content only to: award XP, apply the automod filters chosen by the server, and post deleted or edited messages in the server's log channel. Message content is not stored in the Bot's database, except: the reason of an automod infraction, a message you report (for the staff), and edited or deleted messages if the server turned on the message log (deleted automatically after 7 days by default, 30 at most).

AI assistant (if the server enables it): the question you ask the assistant (/ia command, AI help channel or mentioning the Bot), the conversation of a ticket the staff asks to summarize, or the topic of an announcement to draft, are sent to Anthropic (provider of the Claude AI model) to generate the answer, together with the rules and FAQ written by the server's admins. The Bot does not keep these texts. Anthropic processes them under its own privacy policy (anthropic.com/legal/privacy).

When a ticket is closed, a transcript (copy of the ticket conversation) is sent to the log channel chosen by the server and by direct message to the person who opened the ticket. It is not kept by the Bot.

The verification captcha is generated on demand and kept in memory for 5 minutes maximum; it is never stored. Premium payments are handled entirely by Discord: the Bot neither sees nor stores any banking data.

3. What the Dashboard does not do

  • It only requests the Discord “identify” and “guilds” scopes: it can't read your messages, write, or act on your behalf.
  • No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties for commercial or advertising purposes.
  • No advertising or tracking cookies: the Dashboard only uses one session cookie needed to log in.

4. Who can access the data

A server's data is visible to that server's authorized members (through the Bot's commands or the Dashboard, which requires the “Manage Server” permission) and to the Bot owner for maintenance. Data is hosted on the machine running the Bot and goes through Discord and Cloudflare, which provide the service.

5. Your rights and deletion

You can request access to, correction of or deletion of your data. A server owner can request deletion of all data for their server. Contact: ….

Removing the Bot from a server stops any new collection for that server. Logging out of the Dashboard immediately deletes your session.

6. Security

Communication with the Dashboard is encrypted (HTTPS). Every Dashboard action checks live that you are allowed to manage the server concerned. The Bot's secrets are never exposed publicly.

7. Changes

This policy may change; the date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be announced on the support server or in the Dashboard.
